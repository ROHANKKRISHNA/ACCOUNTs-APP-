Frequently Asked Questions

How to create GST compliant bill?

Go to Favourites or Transactions, click + Add on Sale Invoice. Select the buyer party, enter product quantity, and thermal/A4 invoice with GST calculation will generate automatically.

Is my data safe and backed up?

Yes, all offline and online transactions auto-sync securely to encrypted cloud storage whenever network connection is restored.

How to adjust positive/negative stock?

Navigate to Items tab, select "Items In" or "Items Out", click edit on any item to update quantity or record direct purchases and sales.
